# FAQ

## Is Lensy free?

Your first 100 audits are free, and you don't need an account.

## Does Lensy audit my whole site?

No. Lensy audits one page per scan. Some checks, such as `llms.txt` and crawler access, look at your site as a whole, but the score is for the page you entered.

## Why was my page rejected?

Lensy only scores documentation pages. If a page looks like a marketing page, a listing page or a login screen, Lensy tells you instead of giving advice that doesn't fit.

A page can also look empty if its content loads with JavaScript. If your site publishes a Markdown version of each page, Lensy reads that instead. See [Quickstart](/lensy-docs/quickstart#what-if-my-docs-load-with-javascript).

## Which AI crawlers does Lensy check?

Lensy checks the major AI crawlers, including GPTBot and PerplexityBot. The report lists every crawler it checked and whether each one is allowed.

## Do you change my docs?

No. Lensy only reads the public page. Your coding agent makes the changes, in your repository, when you ask it to.

## How accurate are the results?

Lensy is in beta. Results are generated automatically and may not always be accurate. Treat them as guidance, not a definitive audit.
